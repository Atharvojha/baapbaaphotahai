    Where do I find this?
    1. Open Google Calendar on a laptop and go to Settings.
    2. Under Settings for my calendars, pick your calendar.
    3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.

    It's read-only, so Papa can see your schedule but can't change it. Treat it like a password. Leave it empty to use the demo IIITH day.
